Frequently Asked Questions

How do parents pay school fees via M-Pesa?
Parents log into their portal and click "Pay via M-Pesa". They can select "Fees" or "Wallet", enter the amount, and an STK Push prompt will be sent directly to their phone. The system automatically updates the school ledger.
Can teachers access the system on their phones?
Yes! RacodCore is designed to be fully mobile-responsive. Teachers can mark attendance, enter grades, and view their timetables from their smartphones while in class.
How do I generate a CBC Report Card?
Class teachers can go to "Class Results", select the exam, and click "Download PDF" to generate a personalized CBC report card with performance trends and teacher remarks for every student.
