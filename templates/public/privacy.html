1. Information We Collect

We collect information that is necessary to operate the Service and to deliver the features your school relies on. This includes:

1.1 Information You Provide

  • School Account Data — School name, email address, phone number, postal address, school logo, motto, and administrative details.
  • Staff Data — Names, emails, phone numbers, national ID numbers, TSC numbers, employment type, and photos for teachers and non-teaching staff.
  • Student Data — Names, admission numbers, UPI (NEMIS) numbers, dates of birth, class levels, photos, guardians, and academic records.
  • Parent / Guardian Data — Names, contact details, national IDs, occupations, relationship to students, and linked children.
  • Financial Data — Invoices, fee structures, payments, expenditures, and related references (M-Pesa receipts, bank slips, etc.).
  • Authentication Data — Email addresses and hashed passwords used for logging in.

1.2 Information Collected Automatically

  • Usage Data — IP address, browser type and version, pages visited, timestamps, time spent on each page, unique device identifiers, and diagnostic data.
  • Cookies & Local Storage — Session identifiers and preferences to keep you logged in and improve your experience.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use the collected information for the following purposes:

  • To provide, operate, and maintain the Service
  • To process transactions and generate invoices, receipts, and fee statements
  • To send SMS and email notifications you or your school have triggered (fee reminders, absence alerts, results, etc.)
  • To generate reports such as attendance summaries, class results, and financial statements
  • To monitor usage and improve features based on real-world patterns
  • To detect, prevent, and address technical issues, fraud, or abuse
  • To comply with legal obligations under Kenyan law

We never sell your data. We do not use your school's data for advertising, profiling, or any purpose outside providing the Service you signed up for.

3. Strict Data Isolation

School MS is built as a multi-tenant platform — meaning multiple schools use the same infrastructure. To protect every school's privacy, we enforce strict data isolation:

  • Every school's data is logically separated at the database level.
  • No user from one school can access another school's data under any circumstance.
  • Our own team does not have routine access to any school's student, parent, or financial data.
  • Any support access is logged and audit-traceable, and only granted with the school's explicit permission when troubleshooting.

4. Legal Basis for Processing

Under the Kenya Data Protection Act (2019), we process personal data on the following legal bases:

  • Contract — to deliver the Service your school has subscribed to.
  • Legal obligation — to comply with applicable Kenyan laws.
  • Legitimate interest — to secure, improve, and troubleshoot our platform.
  • Consent — where you or your school have explicitly agreed to specific uses (e.g., SMS notifications).

5. Cookies & Tracking

We use cookies and similar technologies to keep you logged in, remember preferences, and secure sessions. Cookies are small files stored on your device.

You can set your browser to refuse all cookies or alert you when cookies are being sent. However, if you disable cookies, some features of the Service — including secure logins and dashboards — may not work properly.

6. Analytics

We may use privacy-respecting analytics tools to monitor Service usage and improve performance. Analytics data is aggregate and anonymous — it never identifies individual users or their personal records.

Where third-party analytics (such as Google Analytics) are used, they are configured with IP anonymization and minimal data collection.

7. Data Sharing & Disclosure

We do not share your data with third parties except in the following limited circumstances:

  • With your school's authorization — for example, when linking your account to a payment provider.
  • With service providers — hosting providers, SMS gateways (Africa's Talking), and payment processors (Safaricom M-Pesa), strictly to operate the Service.
  • To comply with legal obligations — when required by Kenyan law, court order, or regulatory authority.
  • To protect rights and safety — to investigate fraud, prevent abuse, or protect the safety of users or the public.

Third-party service providers are contractually bound to keep your data confidential and to use it only for the specific purpose they were engaged for.

8. Data Security

We take the security of your data seriously. Our safeguards include:

  • HTTPS / TLS encryption for all communication between your browser and our servers
  • Encrypted databases at rest
  • Hashed passwords using industry-standard algorithms — we never store plain-text passwords
  • Regular backups with off-site redundancy
  • Access logs and audit trails for sensitive actions
  • Strict role-based access so users only see what they are authorized to see

No method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure. While we use commercially reasonable measures, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Data Retention

We retain your school's data for as long as your subscription is active. If your subscription is cancelled:

  • Your data remains available for export for 30 days after cancellation.
  • After 30 days, your data is permanently deleted from our systems and backups.
  • You can request an immediate export at any time by contacting support.

10. Your Rights

Under the Kenya Data Protection Act (2019), you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Rectify any inaccurate or incomplete data
  • Erase your data (subject to legal retention requirements)
  • Restrict or object to certain processing activities
  • Data portability — receive a copy of your data in a standard format
  • Lodge a complaint with the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner (ODPC)

To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details below. We respond within 30 days of receiving a valid request.

11. Children's Data

School MS processes data about students — many of whom are minors — on behalf of the school. The school is the data controller for student data, and we act as a data processor under the school's instruction.

We do not knowingly collect personal data from children outside of what the school provides for legitimate educational and administrative purposes.

12. Third-Party Links

Our Service may contain links to websites we do not operate (such as HELB, KUCCPS, or NEMIS). We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those sites and strongly advise you to review their privacy policies.

13. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we make material changes, we will notify you by:

  • Posting the updated policy on this page with a new "Last Updated" date
  • Sending an email or in-app notification to affected users

Your continued use of the Service after changes take effect constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
