Help Center

Answers to common questions about School MS. Can't find what you're looking for? Contact us.

How much does School MS cost?
Plans start from free for small schools (up to 50 students) and scale to KES 25,000 per term for large institutions. Visit our landing page to see all plans, or contact us for a custom quote.
Can I try it before paying?
Yes — every new school gets a full-featured 30-day free trial. No credit card required. Set up your school, invite staff, and explore everything at no cost.
Does it work on phones and tablets?
Absolutely. School MS is fully responsive and works on any device with a browser — Android phones, iPhones, tablets, laptops, and desktop computers. No app installation required.
How do parents receive fee statements?
Parents get their own login to view invoices, balances, payment history, and results. You can also send SMS reminders directly from the admin dashboard, and download printable PDF statements.
Is my school's data secure?
Every school's data is strictly isolated — no one from another school, and not even our own team, can access your records. We run on enterprise-grade hosting with daily backups, HTTPS encryption, and audit logs.
Can I accept M-Pesa payments?
Yes. We integrate directly with Safaricom's Daraja API, so parents can pay fees via M-Pesa STK Push or Paybill. Payments are automatically recorded against the correct invoice.
How long does setup take?
Most schools are fully set up within a day. Add classes, upload students, invite teachers, and start invoicing. Our team can help with bulk data import if needed.
Can I import my existing student data?
Yes — you can import students, parents, and staff via CSV. Our support team is happy to help with your first import, no matter the size.
What happens if I need help?
Contact us by email or phone anytime. We respond within 24 hours on business days. Enterprise customers get priority support with a dedicated account manager.
