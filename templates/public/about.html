About RacodCore

Building the digital foundation for every Kenyan school — empowering administrators, teachers, and parents with technology designed for our local reality.

Our Vision

To become the most trusted digital partner for schools across East Africa — enabling every institution, from rural primary schools to urban academies, to run efficiently with modern, affordable, locally-built software.

Our Mission

To deliver a complete, affordable school management platform that handles fees, students, staff, grades, and communication — so schools can focus on teaching and learning, not paperwork.

Built for Kenya

Every feature, from M-Pesa integration to NEMIS support, is designed for how Kenyan schools actually operate.

Affordable

Enterprise-grade features at a fraction of what imported solutions cost — so every school can afford them.

Reliable

Secure multi-tenant architecture with strict data isolation, daily backups, and 99.9% uptime.

Why We Built This

We saw brilliant school administrators drowning in paper ledgers, teachers spending hours on manual grade calculations, and parents standing in long queues just to pay school fees.

We built RacodCore because we believe that Kenyan schools deserve world-class technology. Not software adapted from the West, but a platform born here, understanding our CBC curriculum, our M-Pesa ecosystem, and our unique challenges.

Meet the Founder

Samuel Eusabius

Founder & Lead Developer

I am a passionate web application developer driven by a simple belief: technology should solve real-world problems for everyday people.

My journey into software engineering began with a deep curiosity about how digital systems can transform education. After studying Computer Science at The Eldoret National Polytechnic, I realized that while many software solutions exist, very few are built to understand the specific realities of Kenyan schools — the CBC curriculum, the M-Pesa payment ecosystem, and the need for offline-first accessibility.

I built RacodCore to bridge that gap. My passion is not just writing clean code, but building platforms that give school administrators their time back, empower teachers with better tools, and connect parents to their children's education seamlessly.
