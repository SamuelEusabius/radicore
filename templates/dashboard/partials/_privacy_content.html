1. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide (school details, staff and student records, financial data) and information collected automatically (IP address, browser type, page views).

2. How We Use Your Data

Data is used to provide and improve the Service, process transactions, send notifications, and comply with legal obligations under the Kenya Data Protection Act (2019).

3. Strict Data Isolation

Each school's data is strictly isolated. No user from another school — and no member of our team — can access your data without your explicit permission.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell your data. We share it only with service providers (hosting, SMS, payment processors) strictly for operating the Service, or when required by law.

5. Data Security

We use HTTPS encryption, encrypted databases, hashed passwords, regular backups, and strict role-based access controls.

6. Your Rights

Under the Kenya Data Protection Act, you have the right to access, rectify, or erase your data. Contact privacy@schoolms.co.ke to exercise these rights.

7. Data Retention

Your data is retained while your subscription is active and for 30 days after cancellation, then permanently deleted.

8. Children's Data

Student data is processed on behalf of the school. The school is the data controller; School MS acts as a data processor.
